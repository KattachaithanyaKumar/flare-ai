# Flare AI

Flare AI is your personal AI assistant in the browser. It gives you a simple,
friendly place to ask questions, explore ideas, and get things done through
conversation — no technical setup required.

Think of it as a helpful companion you can talk to whenever you need a hand.
Whether you're brainstorming a new project, summarizing something long,
planning your week, or working through a tricky problem, Flare AI is there to
help you think.

## What you can do with Flare AI

- **Ask anything** — Have a natural conversation with an assistant that keeps
  up with your context.
- **Brainstorm ideas** — Generate names, angles, and fresh directions when
  you're stuck.
- **Summarize text** — Turn long notes or documents into clear, useful points.
- **Get help writing** — Draft, rewrite, or polish content with a few prompts.
- **Plan and organize** — Sort through tasks and build a schedule that works
  for you.
- **Start quickly** — Use ready-made suggestions if you're not sure where to
  begin.

## How it works

1. **Create your account.** Sign up with your name, email, and a password.
   Everything you need is kept in one place.
2. **Open your workspace.** Once you're in, you'll land in a clean chat screen
   built for focus.
3. **Start a conversation.** Type a message and press Enter, or pick one of the
   suggested starting points.
4. **Keep things organized.** Start a new chat whenever you want a fresh
   conversation, and revisit your recent chats from the sidebar.

## Designed to feel simple

Flare AI keeps the experience out of your way. The interface is calm and
uncluttered, with your conversations front and center. A light, warm color
palette and clear typography help you stay focused on what matters: getting a
useful answer.

It works on both desktop and mobile, so you can pick up where you left off on
any device.

## Your account, your space

Your conversations live inside your own account. You can log in from anywhere,
and log out whenever you like. Your name is used to personalize your workspace
so it feels like yours.

## A note on accuracy

Flare AI is a helpful tool, but it isn't perfect. It can occasionally get
things wrong, so it's always worth double-checking important information before
relying on it.

## About the name

A flare is a small spark that lights the way — a signal in the dark. That's the
idea behind Flare AI: a bright, dependable light you can turn to when you need
clarity, ideas, or a little momentum.
